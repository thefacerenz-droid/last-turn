# Aurelia CX

The Aurelia CX uses **Car Concept** by Eric Chadwick of Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH (2024), distributed by Khronos Group under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Coastline's adaptation removes the source plate, emblem and logo textures, bakes and reorients the meshes, straightens and rigs the wheels, makes the steering wheel independently movable, groups draw calls, adjusts the track width and uses new real-time paint, glass and lighting materials. Aurelia CX is the in-game vehicle name.
