Art and licenses

Kuroda RS Club

Socksthecat modeled the BRZ Varis body and cockpit and the Silvia S15 Custom wheels. CryHam adapted the meshes, UVs and baked shading for Stunt Rally. Last Exit scales and rigs the car, removes exterior branding, rebuilds its materials and adds working lights, instruments and cabin fittings. These meshes and textures use Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Body and cabin · Wheel source · Game adaptation · License

Aurelia CX

Eric Chadwick at Darmstadt Graphics Group created Car Concept, distributed by the Khronos Group under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Last Exit adapts the chassis, wheels, steering, materials and lights and removes source emblems and lettering.
