## Rebuild and replace

`pnpm run assets:build` regenerates the original high- and low-detail vehicle and palm GLBs. To rebuild the licensed concept, download `Models/CarConcept/glTF-Binary/CarConcept.glb` from the linked source, install Python and NumPy, and run `python scripts/adapt-concept.py path/to/CarConcept.glb`.

Vehicle coordinates use meters, +Y up, -Z forward, and a ground-level root centered between the axles. Required nodes are `FL`, `FR`, `RL`, `RR` and `Steering`. Wheel hubs must be centered on their rotation axes. Name independently controlled materials `paint`, `head`, `brake`, `turnL`, and `turnR`. Keep dimensions, axle geometry and mass data synchronized with `src/vehicles/catalogue.ts`. Any replacement art needs its own compatible redistribution license and attribution.
